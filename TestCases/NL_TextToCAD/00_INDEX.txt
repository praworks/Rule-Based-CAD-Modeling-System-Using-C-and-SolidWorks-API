NL to CAD test pack

Objective:
Use these prompts to test whether the app can create part models directly from natural language.

Structure:
- 11 files
- 10 prompts per file
- 110 prompts total

Recommended usage:
- Start with 01 and move upward.
- Use one prompt at a time.
- Record whether the app created the part, asked for clarification, or failed.
- For prompts with units, keep the exact wording.
